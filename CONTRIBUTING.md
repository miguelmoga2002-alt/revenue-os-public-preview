# Contributing / feedback

This repository is public for **technical review and product discovery**; **no open-source license has been granted yet**. Please do not assume broad reuse or redistribution rights.

- Report reproducible issues **without credentials, personal data or real CRM exports**.
- For commercial feedback, describe the workflow and estimated cost of CRM administration, not customer identities.
- Issues and pull requests may be discussed, but merge/acceptance does not convey a software license.
- Respect the experimental status of the integration adapters and roadmap for generative AI.
